### MEPC 402: Program Elective III
- **Category**: PE | **Credits**: 3 | **Contact Hours**: 3

#### Objectives
1. To understand the periodic disturbances caused by external excitation, rotational and reciprocating imbalance, and support excitation affect a machine's performance.
2. To acquire the knowledge for vibration analysis of physical bodies.
3. To learn Mathematical Modelling of Mechanical Systems for vibration analysis.
4. To learn methods of Vibration isolation of Mechanical systems under periodic inputs.
