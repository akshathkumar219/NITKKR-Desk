# Hostels

> Official NIT Kurukshetra Hostels List.

As with branches, `Code` is stored in student profiles — treat it as permanent.
`Name` is intentionally blank for the boys' hostels: they're referred to by
number only, and leaving it empty makes the parser fall back to the code, so
nothing downstream breaks. The girls' hostels are referred to by name, not
number, so their `Name` is filled in.
