### CEPC 212: Concrete Technology
- **Category**: PC | **Credits**: 3 | **Contact Hours**: 3

#### Units

##### Unit 1: Visual Soil Classification, water content and specific gravity determination.


##### Unit 2: Grain size analysis - sieve analysis and sedimentation analysis.


##### Unit 3: Atterberg limits determination.


##### Unit 4: Relative Density of Granular Soils.


##### Unit 5: Field density by sand replacement method and core cutter method.


##### Unit 6: Proctor compaction test.


##### Unit 7: Coefficient of permeability of soils.


##### Unit 8: Unconfined compressive strength test.


##### Unit 9: Direct shear test on granular soil sample.


##### Unit 10: Unconsolidated undrained (UU) triaxial shear test of fine-grained soil sample.


#### References
- Soil Testing for Engineers by S Prakash & P K Jain
- Engineering Soil Testing by Lambe
- Engineering Properties of Soils and Their Measurement by J E Bowles
- Compendium of Indian Standards on Soil Engineering, SP: 36 (Part 1) - 1987, BIS
