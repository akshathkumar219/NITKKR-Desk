## Syllabi

### MEPC 208: Dynamics of Machines
- **Category**: PC | **Credits**: 3 | **Contact Hours**: 3

#### Objectives
1. To equip the student with fundamental knowledge of dynamics of machines so that student can perform static and dynamic force analysis.
2. To develop knowledge of analytical and graphical methods for calculating balancing of rotary and reciprocating masses.
3. Develop understanding of flywheel analysis, gyroscopic forces and governors
4. To familiarize with the type of gears, gear trains and brakes

### MEPC 214: Department Elective - I
- **Category**: PE | **Credits**: 3 | **Contact Hours**: 3

#### Units

##### Unit 1: To determine experimentally the ratio of cutting to idle time to the crank & slotted lever (QRM) &
Compare the result with theoretical values. Plot the following: (i) θ v/s displacement of slider (ii) θ v/s velocity (iii) θ v/s acceleration

##### Unit 2: To determine velocity & acceleration of slider in Slider Crank mechanism and plot the following:
(i) θ v/s displacement of slider (ii) θ v/s velocity (iii) θ v/s acceleration Compare the values of velocities and acceleration with those obtained theoretically. Assume angular velocity = 1 rad/sec

##### Unit 3: To determine the values of coefficient of friction between the screw & nut of jack while:
(i) Raising the load (ii) Lowering the load

##### Unit 4: To draw experimentally a curve of the follower displacement v/s cam angle. Differentiate the above
curve to get velocity & acceleration plot & compare the values with those obtained analytically.

##### Unit 5: To determine the value of coefficient of friction between belt & pulley and plot a graph between log
T /T , &θ and measure the slip and creep in belt drive. 1 2

##### Unit 6: To find experimentally the gyroscopic couple on motorized Gyroscope and compare with applied
couple.

##### Unit 7: To find critical speed experimentally and to compare whirling speed of shaft with theoretical values.


##### Unit 8: To perform the experiment of balancing of rotating parts and find unbalanced couples and force.


##### Unit 9: To study various types of Gears & their terminologies – Spur, Helical, Bevel and Worm Gear. To
study the various gear trains: simple, compound, reverted & epicyclic.

##### Unit 10: To Plot the characteristic curves for Porter, Proell and Hartnell governors. To perform the effect of
varying the mass of central sleeve for Porter and Proell governors.

##### Unit 11: To find experimentally the Coriolli's component of acceleration and compare with theoretical
values.

##### Unit 12: To measure Epicyclic Gear ratio between input shaft and output shaft.
